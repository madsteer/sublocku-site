SubLocKu

Privacy Policy

Effective December 1, 2026 · iPhone app

The short version

We don't collect any information about you. There are no accounts and no developer servers, and your games stay on your iPhone. The free version shows ads from Google, and Google's ad software receives some device data to do that. Subscribers see no ads.

01

Introduction

SubLocKu ("the App", "we", "our") is a block puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains what information the App keeps, what leaves your device and why, and what we never collect.

The App has no user accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of our own. We have no server that receives data from it.

02

Information We Do Not Collect

We, the developer, never collect, receive or store:

  • Your name, email address or any other contact details
  • Accounts, passwords or sign-in information
  • Your games, scores or daily puzzle results
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Payment or billing information
  • Precise location, contacts, photos or anything else from your iPhone

We are not able to identify who you are or see how you play.

The one exception to "nothing leaves your iPhone" is advertising in the free version. Google's ad software receives some device data directly; we never see it. Sections 04 and 05 explain exactly what.
03

The App stores the following only on your device. None of it is sent to us.

05

Advertising

If you don't subscribe, the App shows a full-screen ad between games, never during one. Google may combine data from the App with data from other apps and sites that use its services.

The App does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Unless you have allowed tracking elsewhere, iOS keeps your advertising identifier hidden and Google shows ads that are less personalized.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before ads are personalized. You can change your answer at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices in the App.

06

Subscriptions & Payments

The optional "remove ads" subscription is sold and processed entirely by Apple.

  • Apple handles all payment data
  • We never see your card details, name or Apple Account
  • The App only learns whether you have an active subscription

All payment data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

07

Third-Party Software

The App includes exactly two pieces of third-party software, both from Google and both used only for ads in the free version:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), which shows the ads
  • Google User Messaging Platform, which asks for consent where required

The App contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software of our own. Apple may share anonymous App Store statistics and crash reports with us if you've opted in to sharing with developers in your iPhone's settings; that is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

08

Children's Privacy

SubLocKu is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

09

Your Choices & Deleting Your Data

  • Subscribe to remove ads and stop all requests to Google.
  • Limit ad tracking in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Review data Google uses for ads at My Ad Center.
  • Delete the App to erase everything it stored on your iPhone.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, correct or delete on request.

10

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date at the top. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the revised policy.
